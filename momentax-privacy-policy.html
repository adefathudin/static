Privacy Policy

Ade Fathudin ("we", "our", or "us") built the MomentaX application as a Freemium app with optional Pro subscriptions and in-app purchases. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decides to use our application.

By using MomentaX, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We take your privacy seriously and strive to ensure that your personal moments, memories, and personal records remain private and secure.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with reminder alerts, dual Gregorian-Hijri calculations, cloud synchronization, and backup features, we may collect certain information:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you sign in using your Google Account, we receive your basic profile details including your name, email address, profile picture URL, and Google user identifier. If you choose to use the app in Guest Mode, no Google account information is transmitted.
  • Moments, Memories & Event Data: Information you voluntarily enter into the app, such as memorial titles, event dates, relationship labels, event categories (e.g. Birthdays, Memorials/Haul, Anniversaries, Keepsakes), prayer/notes text, and notification reminder schedules.
  • Photos and Media: Images and photos you attach to your moments. Photos are stored locally on your device storage and, if cloud sync is active, uploaded securely to your private cloud storage bucket.
  • Purchase & Entitlement Data: When you subscribe to MomentaX Pro or make an in-app purchase, transaction validation is handled via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or sensitive financial credentials.
  • Device and Diagnostic Information: For non-pro users, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and performance analytics to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and deliver accurate annual reminder alarms (exact alarm & calendar notifications).
  • To synchronize your personal moments across your devices via Google Firebase.
  • To enable seamless encrypted backups to your personal Google Drive account.
  • To unlock and maintain your VIP / Pro ad-free membership status.
  • To display relevant ads through Google AdMob for free tier users.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

MomentaX integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide core app functionality. Below are links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage)
  • Google AdMob (Mobile Ads)
  • RevenueCat (In-App Purchases & Subscriptions)
  • Google Drive API (App Data Backup)

4. User Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Your Right to Erase All Data: You have full control over your personal data at all times. We provide both an in-app deletion mechanism and a direct contact option.

Method 1: In-App Instant Deletion

  1. Open the MomentaX application.
  2. Tap the menu / hamburger icon to open the Navigation Drawer.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account & Data" (Hapus Akun & Data).
  4. Type the confirmation keyword (DELETE or HAPUS) and confirm.

Upon confirmation, all your cloud moments stored in Cloud Firestore, local user data, RevenueCat identity, and your Firebase Authentication profile are immediately and permanently erased.

Method 2: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at adefathudin76@gmail.com with the subject "Delete MomentaX Account Request" from the Google email address registered with the app. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

5. Device Permissions

MomentaX requests the following permissions strictly when necessary:

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to alert you on the day of or days prior to important moments.
  • Photos & Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Camera): Required only if you choose to attach photos to your moments.
  • Internet: Required for Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore sync, Google Drive backup, and Google Mobile Ads.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us your personal information. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit, AES-256 for local backup files, and authenticated Firebase rules). However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

MomentaX is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we immediately delete it from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting to this page.
